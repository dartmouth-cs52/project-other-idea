# Two-factor authentication

With two-factor authentication, signing in takes a password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app. The workspace owner can require it for everyone in the workspace.

To turn the requirement on:

1. Press the round "A" button at the top right and choose Settings.
2. Press the Security tab.
3. Tick Require two-factor authentication, at the top of the tab.

There is nothing to save. The change applies as soon as you tick the box, and the page shows "Two-factor authentication is now required."

Every teammate, the owner included, is then asked to set up two-factor authentication at their next sign-in. Nobody is signed out when you tick the box. People who are already signed in carry on until their session ends, which is at most 30 days later. Anyone who joins the workspace afterwards sets it up the first time they sign in.

Any authenticator app that produces 6-digit codes will work. Codes by text message are not supported. During setup each person is given 10 backup codes, and each backup code works once.

If someone loses the device that holds their authenticator app, they can sign in with a backup code. If they have no backup codes left, the owner can write to support to have that person's two-factor setup reset.

To stop requiring it, untick the box. The page shows "Two-factor authentication is no longer required." and teammates are no longer asked for a code, starting with their next sign-in.
