# Roles and removing people

The Team page lists the people who can access the workspace. Each of them has one of two roles: owner or member.

The owner is the person who created the workspace. A workspace has exactly one owner, and the list on the Team page shows "(owner)" after their name. Everyone else is a member, and anyone who accepts an invite joins as a member.

Members can create projects, deploy to any project in the workspace, invite teammates and see the workspace's usage on the Overview page.

Some things are for the owner only. Only the owner can remove people, change the plan, update the payment method, change the workspace name and time zone, turn the two-factor authentication requirement on or off, and create API keys.

Only the workspace owner can remove people. To remove someone, go to Team and press Remove next to their name.

The person loses access straight away and is signed out of Acme Console within a minute. Projects they created, and everything they deployed, stay in the workspace and keep running. Their place on the plan is free at once, so you can invite someone else.

The owner cannot be removed. A member cannot remove another member, and cannot remove themselves; they need to ask the owner.

Someone who has been removed can be invited again later. They need a new invite, and they come back as a member.
