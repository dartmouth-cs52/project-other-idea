# Getting around Acme Console

Acme Console is where teams manage their Acme projects, teammates and subscription.

Every page has the same bar at the top. On the left is the name Acme Console, which takes you to the Overview page. Next to it are three links: Overview, Projects and Team. At the top right is a round "A" button.

Overview is the first page you see. It says how many projects and teammates the workspace has, and it has two cards: Usage this month and Latest deploy.

Projects lists the workspace's projects and is where you create new ones. Team lists the people who can access the workspace and is where you invite more.

Everything about the account (settings, plan, payment, security) is under the round "A" button at the top right. Press it to open a menu with three choices: Settings, Plan and billing, and Sign out. Press the button again to close the menu.

Settings has three tabs. General holds the workspace name and time zone. Plan and billing holds your plan, payment method and invoices; it is the same page that Plan and billing in the menu opens. Security holds the two-factor authentication requirement and API keys.

Sign out ends your session in the browser you are using. Other browsers where you are signed in are not affected. If you do not sign out, you stay signed in on that browser for 30 days, and then you are asked to sign in again.

Acme Console works in the current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Some changes can only be made by the workspace owner; the article Roles and removing people says which.
