# Inviting teammates

Anyone in a workspace can invite a new teammate. All you need is the person's email address.

1. Press Team in the bar at the top of the page.
2. Below the list of people, type the person's address in the Email address field.
3. Press Send invite.

The page shows "Invite sent." and the field is emptied, ready for the next address.

The person gets an email with a link that works for 7 days. They must accept with the same email address the invite was sent to. When they accept, they join the workspace as a member and their name appears in the list on the Team page. Until then they are not listed, and they do not count towards the number of people your plan allows.

If the link has run out, or the email never arrived, send the invite again: type the same address and press Send invite. The person gets a new link that works for another 7 days, and the earlier link stops working. You can re-send as many times as you need.

If the workspace already has as many people as its plan allows, the person cannot accept until a place is free or the workspace moves to a larger plan. The number of people each plan allows is in the article Usage and limits.

Only the workspace owner can remove people. The article Roles and removing people explains what members and the owner can each do.
