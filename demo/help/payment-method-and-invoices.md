# Payment method and invoices

Billing details are on the Plan and billing page. Press the round "A" button at the top right and choose Plan and billing.

The Payment method section shows the type of card and its last four digits, for example "Visa ending in 4242". We don't store full card numbers; our payment provider holds them.

Billing details are changed with Update payment method. Only the workspace owner can do this.

1. Press Update payment method.
2. In the dialog, fill in Name on card and Billing postcode. Both are required.
3. Press Save payment method. Press Cancel to leave things as they were.

The page shows "Payment method updated." The new details are used from the next charge. If an earlier charge failed, we try it again within an hour of you saving.

The dialog does not ask for a card number. To pay with a different card, the owner writes to support and we send a secure form from our payment provider, which works once and for 24 hours. A workspace has one payment method at a time. We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express.

The Invoices section is below. Invoices are emailed on the 1st of each month, to the workspace owner's email address, and each one covers the month that is starting. Press Download latest invoice to get the most recent one. Earlier invoices are not listed on the page. They are in the monthly emails, and support can send a copy of any invoice from the last 12 months.

An invoice carries the workspace name, the name on the card and the billing postcode as they were on the day it was issued.
