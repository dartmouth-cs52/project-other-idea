# Workspace name and time zone

The workspace name and time zone are on the General tab of Settings. Only the workspace owner can change them.

1. Press the round "A" button at the top right and choose Settings. Settings opens on the General tab.
2. Type a new name in Workspace name, or pick a zone from the Time zone list: UTC, Eastern or Pacific.
3. Press Save changes.

The page shows "Settings saved." Nothing changes until you press Save changes.

The workspace name is what teammates see in invite emails and what is printed at the top of invoices. Renaming the workspace does not affect your projects, their web addresses, your API keys or your teammates. The new name is used from the moment you save. Invoices that were already issued keep the old name.

The time zone changes the times shown on deploys and invoices: the time in each deploy email, and the dates on each invoice. There is one time zone for the whole workspace, so every teammate sees the same times. A new workspace starts on UTC. Eastern and Pacific follow daylight saving time automatically.

The time zone only changes how times are written. It does not move when things happen: usage still resets, and invoices are still issued, at midnight UTC on the 1st of each month.

The Latest deploy card on the Overview page says how long ago a deploy went live, such as "2 hours ago", so it reads the same in every time zone.
