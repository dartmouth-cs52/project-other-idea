# Plans and billing

Your plan is on the Plan and billing page. Press the round "A" button at the top right and choose Plan and billing, or press the Plan and billing tab in Settings. The Current plan section names the plan you are on and what it costs a month.

There are three plans: Starter, Team and Business. Starter is free. Team is $49 a month. Business is $199 a month. Each price is for the whole workspace, however many people are in it, and does not include tax. What each plan includes is listed in the article Usage and limits.

Only the workspace owner can change the plan, with the Change plan button in the Current plan section.

Moving to a larger plan takes effect straight away. The difference for the days left in the month is added to your next invoice. Moving to a smaller plan takes effect on the 1st of the next month, and you keep the larger plan until then. To stop paying, move to Starter.

Paid plans are charged in advance, on the 1st of each month, to the payment method shown on the Plan and billing page.

The first payment on a paid plan is refunded in full if the owner asks within 14 days of the charge. After that, payments are not refunded, including for part of a month. To ask for a refund, the owner writes to support.

If the charge on the 1st fails, the owner is emailed and the invoice must be paid within 10 days, by the 11th of the month. If it is still unpaid on the 11th, deploys are paused and requests to your projects are refused until it is paid.
