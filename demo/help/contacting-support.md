# Contacting support

You can reach Acme support by email at support@acme.example. A person reads every message.

Support is open Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 UTC, and is closed on 25 December and 1 January. We reply within one business day. Workspaces on the Business plan get a first reply within 4 working hours.

So that we can help in our first reply, tell us your workspace name, as it appears in Workspace name on the General tab of Settings. Add the project involved, if there is one, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and when it happened.

Some requests are only accepted from the workspace owner's email address, because they change who or what can reach the workspace or how it is paid for. These are: switching off an API key, resetting a teammate's two-factor authentication, paying with a different card, and anything about refunds or unpaid invoices. If a member writes about one of these, we ask them to have the owner contact us.

Support will never ask for your password, an API key's secret value, a two-factor code or a full card number. If a message asks for any of these, do not answer it. Forward it to us instead.

There are things support cannot do. We cannot read an API key's secret value, and we cannot change a project's region or its name.
