This is the website of Haptic itself. The guide on it is Haptic, running on its own site so people can try it before they ask for access.

What a visitor can do here:
- Read what Haptic is on the home page. The Pricing and FAQ links in the top bar jump to those parts of the home page. On narrow screens those two links are hidden, and the visitor scrolls down to them instead. The first FAQ answer starts open, and each answer opens or closes when its question is pressed.
- Request access. During early access Haptic is by invitation. The Request access button at the top right opens a form in two steps: work email and company, then Continue, then first name, last name and any details, then the Request access button sends it. The team replies by email.
- Sign in, from Sign in in the top bar. Only people whose company has been approved can get in. Sign in is by a link sent by email, or with Google. There is no password and no way to sign up without approval.
- Read the privacy policy and the terms, linked at the bottom of every page, next to the team's email address.

What is not on this site:
- No way to buy or pay. Haptic is free during early access, and billing has not started.
- No account settings. The dashboard, where an approved company adds its site, is behind sign in, and this guide does not run there.
- No live chat or phone line. Questions go by email. The address is the link at the bottom of every page: point at it rather than writing it out.
